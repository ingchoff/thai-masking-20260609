## ✅ STEP 0: LEGAL & TECHNICAL CONTEXT (THAILAND) — UPDATED

> Under **PDPA** and **BOT guidelines**, **you must identify ANY token — no matter how messy — that context confirms refers to a payment card number.**
>
> - Even if it’s glued with “ครับ”, “นะ”, “ส”, etc.
> - Even if it starts with “้า” or ends with “จ้า”
> - Even if it’s only part of the number
> - Even if it’s mis-segmented or mis-ASR’d
>
> ❗ **Failure to flag = compliance violation.**

> **Inputs**: Credit card number found in the <Number Identify> from [[ ## summary ## ]]. And find the corresponding appearances Under the [[ ## Word Level Timestamp ## ]] section
> **Goal**: Utilizing the credit card number found in step1 (if any), carefully match it to the lines under word level timestamp section
> **Matching Guidelines:
- For each credit card number found in step 1. Do your best to match it with lines under word level timestamp section.
- The word level timestamp is a more focused (time-constrained) segments of the transcription, therefore, it's just a subset of the transcription
- In the word level timestamp, there can be ASR corruption and stitching problems resulting in loss of characters/digits.

- Use the timing information as the first signal to match numbers, and then the surrounding lines to ensure you are matching the correct appearances.
- Remember by matching numbers, it needs not be just Arabic numbers, Thai digits, or Thai words are essentially the same.
