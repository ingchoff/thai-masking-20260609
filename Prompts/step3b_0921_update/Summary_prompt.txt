## 🧩 STEP 1: Understanding and summarizing transcription

> **Input**: A snippet of transcription is given in [[ ## Transcription ## ]]
> **Goal**: Understand the content of the transcription accurately. Then provide the summary of it.
> **Summary Focus**:
- Identify the intention of the transcription, especially revolving the numbers sequence that are mentioned
- Surgically identify and list out the important number sequences that are mentioned in the transcription and the "meaning" of the said numbers.
- Categorize the meaning into one of these (Bear in mind that ASR error or incorrect splitting of transcription chunks may affect the completeness of the numbers):
- credit card number: the 16 digits (or partial) credit card numbers. In Thailand Lotus card is also a credit card. Categorize ATM/debit card into this category. << This is the ONLY PAN related categories
- expiry date: the expiration date of the credit card numbers.
- CVV/CVS: the security code of the credit card numbers
- National-ID: Thailand national ID (13 digits long), also known as citizen ID "บัตรบาลชน"
- Phone Number: Any contact number provided, can be phone number, service center number, etc..
- Address: numbers of addresses, like pos code, street/blocks number, etc..
- Other: other numbers that are not in the above categories.
- For each identified number, attach the time of which it appeared. Just use the transcription time as provided. If the number appeared more than once. List it all out.

Guidelines:
- Understanding the semantic flows are key for accurate intention understanding.
- The order of semantic context is important clue to help identify the intention. *Which means understanding both before and after the numbers are important*
example:
- Here is the credit card number, [1234,and then, 3456, ...] -> The numbers are clearly credit card number.
- This is the card number, [1234, 5678, ...]. Thank you for providing the card number, can I confirmed that it is the citizen card number of Mrs. Eve? -> Even at first it says card number, however later on it confirmed that it is referring to a citizen number. So this is National-ID
- What's the expiry date? It's 0511. 0512? ok thank you for providing the number, now I repeat 123445676.... -> In this case, the 12344... is likely credit card number. But the 0511 should be expiry date. and 0512 is likely a misheard of 0511 thus it's also expiry date.
